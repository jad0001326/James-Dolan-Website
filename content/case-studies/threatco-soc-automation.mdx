## Problem

Day-to-day SOC work depended on manual steps spread across SOAR, SIEM, and EDR platforms. That added workload, and detection and response logic was harder to improve when the path from alert to action was not written down as a playbook.

## Approach

Develop and optimise playbooks across those platforms. Automate key SOC processes. Analyse large-scale security data to spot trends, improve detection logic, and decide what was worth automating next. Support the tool integrations that let those systems work together, and keep structured playbook documentation so the team could share the work and keep it running. Delivery was aligned with the security and business teams who relied on the workflows.

## Outcome

The published result of that work was a lower manual workload and more efficient operations, with detection and response that the team could repeat.
