## Problem

Several client environments were moving off Palo Alto Cortex XSOAR and onto D3 Security (D3 Smart SOAR). Playbooks, integrations, and the way analysts moved through an incident had to be rebuilt on the destination platform without dropping the response process during the change.

## Approach

Support the migration across multiple clients. Rebuild the response workflows on D3, including the integrations those playbooks depend on, and keep the documentation aligned with the new platform so analysts and engineers are working from the same version.
